About iVOTE

Democratizing decision-making through secure, transparent, and user-friendly digital voting.

Our Mission

iVOTE was built on a simple premise: voting should be accessible, secure, and modern. We aim to replace outdated and inefficient physical paper ballots with a state-of-the-art online election system that doesn't compromise on transparency or security.

Whether for student associations, professional organizations, corporate boards, or local community clubs, iVOTE provides standard-setting voter turnout, real-time auditability, and immediate result publication.

Core Values

We hold ourselves to the highest standards to maintain the absolute trust of our users. Our platform is continuously improved around three pillars:

Absolute Security

End-to-end security measures ensure your credentials, participant list, and votes are heavily protected.

Uncompromising Transparency

Candidates and organizers can request cryptographic audit trails to verify election results in real-time.

Inclusive Accessibility

Cast your ballot from anywhere, at any time, using any internet-connected device in just a few clicks.

How it Works

iVOTE keeps voter verification and vote casting strictly separate to ensure complete ballot secrecy. While the system tracks who has voted to prevent double-voting, your actual choice is anonymized and stored securely in a separate ledger, maintaining total privacy.
