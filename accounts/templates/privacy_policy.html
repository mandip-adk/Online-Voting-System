Privacy Policy

How we collect, protect, and use your data to maintain a secure and anonymous voting environment.

Last Updated: June 29, 2026

Information We Collect

To provide a secure and reliable voting platform, we collect a limited amount of personal data:

  • Account Information: Your username, full name, email address, and role choice (Voter or Organizer) when you register.
  • Verification Identifier: A unique identifier (e.g., student ID, employee number, or phone number) to verify eligibility and prevent duplicate voter registrations.
  • Participation Logs: We record the list of elections in which you have voted to enforce the "one person, one vote" rule.

Complete Ballot Anonymity

We employ a strict cryptographic separation of identity and vote records:

  • Decoupled Votes: When you cast a ballot, the vote is stored anonymously in a separate database table. The connection between your user account and the specific selection is immediately discarded.
  • No Tracking: Neither election organizers, administrators, nor other voters can see who you voted for.

Data Security

We secure your data using industry-standard protocols, including SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit, secure database hashing for user passwords, and restricted administrative permissions.

Contact Our Privacy Team

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your voter data is managed, please contact us at privacy@ivote.com or visit our Contact Us page.
