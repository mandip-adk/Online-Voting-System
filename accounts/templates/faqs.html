Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about the iVOTE online election platform, voter eligibility, and security verification.

To vote in any election, you must register by creating an account. Click the Register button in the upper-right corner of the home page. Provide your username, full name, email address, a unique ID (e.g. Student ID, Citizenship No., or Phone Number), and select your role as a Voter. Once registered, you will be able to access your voter dashboard.

Yes, confidentiality and integrity are the core principles of iVOTE. When you cast a ballot, your voting token is decoupled from your profile, creating an anonymous entry in the votes database. While the system registers that you have participated in an election to prevent double voting (Voter Participation tracking), it is cryptographically impossible to link your identity to the specific candidate you voted for.

If you want to host an election, register with the role Organizer. On your dashboard, you will have access to an intuitive panel where you can create new elections, specify start/end dates, set eligibility configurations (open to all, restricted by email domains, or restricted by unique voter ID lists), and approve or reject candidate applications.

To guarantee absolute transparency, candidates can submit an **Audit Request** for their elections. This request invokes a verification scan of all cryptographic voting tokens generated during the election. An administrator reviews the logs to confirm that all cast votes align with individual voter participation logs, ensuring that no votes were manipulated, injected, or lost.

Yes! For active and closed elections, you can view real-time scorecards and percentage metrics on your Voter Dashboard or Organizer Dashboard. The system dynamically updates the results as votes are cast. Once an election is closed, the final tally is locked and certified for publication.
